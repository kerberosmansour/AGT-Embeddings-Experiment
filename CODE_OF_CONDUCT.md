# Code Of Conduct

This project welcomes careful, good-faith technical disagreement.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and direct.
- Critique claims, evidence, and methods rather than people.
- Assume contributors may be working across different machines and time zones.
- Keep discussions focused on AGT prompt-injection research and reproducibility.
- Make uncertainty explicit when a result is preliminary or bounded.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private data, credentials, or sensitive reports in issues or pull
  requests.
- Repeatedly presenting research evidence as production assurance after being
  corrected.
- Deliberately misrepresenting metrics, thresholds, or validation scope.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate
this code. Serious or repeated issues may result in a temporary or permanent
ban from project spaces.

For conduct concerns, open a GitHub issue with the label `conduct` and include
only the minimum context needed for maintainers to understand the issue.
